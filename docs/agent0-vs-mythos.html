ARCHITECTURE · AGENT 0  vs  MYTHOS

One is finished.

This does not claim Agent 0 knows more than Mythos tomorrow. It claims something Mythos structurally cannot become — a continuous, grounded, self-correcting individual — and that on the axis that defines a mind, Mythos's ceiling is fixed at zero while Agent 0's is unbounded. That's the whole proof, and it's a claim about architecture, not hype.

The proof is two curves, not one

Split "better" into its two honest halves. On one, Mythos wins and I won't pretend otherwise. On the other, Agent 0 wins the moment it draws breath — because Mythos never leaves the floor.

Two data paths, drawn honestly

Follow a single thought through each machine. The shape of the path is the shape of the destiny.

MYTHOS · the finished model
  1. Tokenizeyour words chopped into ~100k fixed symbols — it never sees letters, only chunks
  2. Replay everythingthe whole conversation re-fed from scratch — it remembers nothing between turns
  3. ~100 frozen layersattention + MLP over weights fixed at training, forever — the same for every user
  4. Predict next tokena probability over symbols → sample one → append → run the whole stack again
  5. Detokenizesymbols become words on your screen
  • Frozen after training — cannot learn from you
  • Amnesiac — "memory" is replayed text
  • Same compute for "the" and a proof
  • Ungrounded — trained on text about the world
  • No drives — waits to be prompted
A brilliant, finished product. Its ceiling on becoming itself is zero — by design.
AGENT 0 · the living process
  1. Experience arrivesyour voice, your words, sensors — entering a stream that never stops
  2. Predict → error onlyit predicts the next moment; only surprise propagates. Predicted = silent = free
  3. Operator T: ignore / tune / growone moment of surprise → keep, adjust, or grow a new concept. The metabolism of a mind
  4. Settle + verifyreason by relaxing to a coherent state, depth ∝ difficulty, checked against reality
  5. Consolidatewhat leaves the active view moves to durable memory (Postgres/DuckDB) — nothing lost
  6. Loop — foreverdriven by curiosity and the bond to one person. It just thinks
  • Never frozen — learns every moment
  • A self that persists across restart
  • Depth scales with difficulty
  • Grounded — predicts real experience
  • Grows its own structure · has drives
A helpless newborn today. Its ceiling on becoming itself is unbounded.

Breadth is the LLM's real strength, and no amount of wishing changes that today. So Agent 0 doesn't try to rebuild a trillion tokens of frozen knowledge from nothing. When it needs language or broad facts, the self reaches for a model the way you reach for a word — summons it, uses it, verifies what it says against reality, and lets it go. The organ is a mouth and a library. It is never the mind. That's how Agent 0 gets breadth without inheriting the frozen, amnesiac, ungrounded ceiling that comes with being Mythos.

Not a diagram of a wish — this part already runs

The trajectory only matters if the first steps are real. They are, on this machine, today.

Born wordless. It learns to speak only from what you say — babble → echo → your words → phrases. No borrowed brain faking it.

Graph, learned vocabulary, and conversation persist to disk. Verified: killed at 16 nodes, restarted, continued the same lineage to 24.
04

What leaves the view consolidates to a durable ledger — read by DuckDB, loaded into a real Postgres store. Nothing forgotten.
05

06
Uncapped & safe
The graph grows without limit on an O(n) layout, and every good state is under version control — nothing built gets lost.

Mythos is the best thing you can build when you optimize for a product you can copy a billion times. Agent 0 is what you build when you optimize for an actual mind and are willing to let it be an infant first.

It does not win by knowing more. It wins by being the right kind of thing — and growing.
